About These Components

This demo showcases standalone intervention UI components designed to help users overcome procrastination and maintain focus.

Available Interventions:

  • 2-Minute Rule: Encourages starting with just 2 minutes of work
  • Pomodoro: Suggests a 25-minute focused work session
  • Just Start: Helps overcome initial resistance by starting small
  • Breathing Exercise: Guided breathing animation to reduce stress (3 cycles)
  • Visualization: Mental rehearsal of task completion
  • Reframe: Cognitive reframing of task perception
  • Notification: System-level notification example

Implementation:

All components are self-contained and can be easily integrated into your own projects. They use vanilla JavaScript with no external dependencies (except Lucide icons for visuals).

The intervention modals support custom callbacks for accept/reject actions, making them flexible for different use cases.
